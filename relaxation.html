    Keep it relaxed

    Reward calm behavior after each successful task. Sitting or lying down is fine. Repeat a day as needed; finishing a timer doesn't mean your dog is ready to move on. If your dog becomes uneasy, reduce the distance, movement or sound, return to an easier exercise, or stop.

    Timed movements wait for you to start the timed part. Tasks with several timed parts run each part separately. After a movement, confirm that you've returned and finished before treating. Keep this page visible; switching tabs pauses the session.

    Read the full protocol and task-specific notes (PDF) before starting. Task sequence and durations: Karen L. Overall, 1997 / 2013, task sheets on pages 15–31. This independent timer is a companion to the protocol.
